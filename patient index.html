Test Name

Uric Acid Test

Purpose

The Uric Acid Test measures the level of uric acid in the blood. It helps diagnose gout, kidney stones, and kidney-related disorders.

Sample Required

Blood Sample

Preparation Instructions

Fasting for 4–8 hours may be recommended before the test. Follow your doctor's instructions.

Normal Reference Range

Male: 3.4 – 7.0 mg/dL
Female: 2.4 – 6.0 mg/dL

Report Delivery Time

Within 24 Hours

Important Instructions

Avoid alcohol and high-purine foods such as red meat and seafood before the test.

Fasting Requirements

Fasting: Recommended for 4–8 Hours

* Normal reference values may vary depending on the laboratory and patient condition.
